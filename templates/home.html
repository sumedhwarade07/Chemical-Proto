Mechanism of Detergent Action

Tri-Component Synergy

{{ content.site.theory }}

1. Surfactants

Amphiphilic molecules featuring hydrophilic headgroups and hydrophobic tail chains. They orient at grease interfaces, encapsulating soils into water-soluble spherical micelles.

2. Alkaline Builders

Sodium carbonate sequesters hard-water Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺ cations by precipitating insoluble mineral flakes, preserving surfactant molecules from inactivation.

3. Hydrotropic Solvents

Distilled water and hydrotropes (SCS) provide an ion-free medium that disrupts excessive self-association, guaranteeing permanent solution optical clarity and cloud-point elevation.
